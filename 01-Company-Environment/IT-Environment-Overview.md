# Northstar Technologies — IT Environment Overview

## 1. Organization Overview

Northstar Technologies is a mid-sized business services organization with approximately 120 employees. The company operates from a primary Toronto office and supports a hybrid workforce, including employees who work remotely.

The internal IT team provides technical support for employees, endpoints, business applications, identity and access, connectivity, and day-to-day IT operations.

This project simulates the responsibilities of an IT Support Analyst working within this environment.

## 4. Identity and Access Environment

Northstar uses centralized identity and access management for employee accounts.

The simulated environment includes:

* Active Directory user accounts
* Security groups
* Department-based access
* User onboarding and offboarding
* Password resets
* Account lockout investigation
* Access and permission troubleshooting

Access is provided according to the employee's department and job responsibilities.

IT Support follows the principle of least privilege and escalates access changes requiring elevated authorization.

## 9. Support Escalation Model

### Level 1 — IT Support

Handles common endpoint, application, account, connectivity, and hardware incidents.

### Level 2 — Systems / Infrastructure

Handles issues requiring deeper investigation or administrative changes involving infrastructure, servers, networking, identity, or permissions.

### Level 3 — Specialized / Vendor Support

Handles highly specialized infrastructure, application, hardware, ISP, or vendor-specific issues.

Issues are escalated when they exceed the analyst's access level, technical scope, or authorization.

## 10. Project Scope

This portfolio project simulates the day-to-day work of an IT Support Analyst within the Northstar Technologies environment.

The project will demonstrate:

* Incident management
* Technical troubleshooting
* Windows endpoint support
* Identity and access management
* Network diagnostics
* Hardware and asset management
* Microsoft 365 support scenarios
* PowerShell-based support automation
* Knowledge-base development
* Incident investigation
* Root-cause analysis
* Preventive and corrective actions

Where enterprise services require paid subscriptions, the project uses local hands-on labs, controlled simulations, or documented support scenarios rather than claiming access to a live production environment.

---

## 11. Project Objective

The objective is to demonstrate a structured, evidence-based approach to IT support and systems operations.

The analyst is expected not only to resolve incidents, but also to:

1. Identify the business impact.
2. Gather relevant evidence.
3. Troubleshoot systematically.
4. Determine the most likely root cause.
5. Apply an appropriate resolution.
6. Validate service restoration.
7. Document the incident.
8. Identify preventive actions where appropriate.

This approach reflects practical IT Support and Systems Operations workflows rather than isolated technical exercises.
